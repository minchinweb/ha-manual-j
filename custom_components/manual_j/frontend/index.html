Design conditions

Rooms: calculated against fitted

Heat loss and cooling are Manual J at the design conditions, from House Structure's areas and the nominal R-values (the house defaults). UA calc is the room's calculated conductance (walls, roofs, windows and infiltration). UA fitted is Home Thermal Lab's zone model's measured cooling rate (when it is installed) turned into the same units, so a ratio near 1 means the nominal envelope matches the house. The envelope scale is the factor on the nominal conductances that would match the fit: above 1 means more heat flows than the nominal R-values give. Walls below grade are left out of the fit, which has its own ground term.
